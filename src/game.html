HOW TO PLAY

STICKSlide left or right to walk, down to duck. You face the way you last pushed, so turn toward an opponent before you attack or block. JJump. Steer with the stick while you're in the air, and press P or K for a jump kick. P, KPunch and kick. Land a hit, then tap P or K again right away to chain a combo. Punches only land where the body really is: they sail over small fighters (Haddie, Harper, Slime) and over anyone ducking. Kicks, the low jab (duck + punch), Specials and Supers hit everyone. BTap or hold to block. Blocked hits only chip a little health. Double-tap B right next to an opponent who isn’t attacking or blocking to throw them. ROLLHold B and push the stick left or right to roll, even right past an opponent. You can’t be hit in the middle of a roll, but you can at the start and end, and a Super still catches you. To turn around while blocking, let go of B first. SPECIALFree to use: after each one, the SPECIAL button fills back up. Light ones like the Goblin's dagger and Imp's fireball come back fast; big ones like AC's dumbbell take longer. One out at a time, and it stops at the first fighter it hits. Jump or block the dumbbell, and jump, duck or block Becky's coffee mug. SUPERLand and take hits to fill your meter. When it’s full, SUPER lights up. Supers can’t be blocked, jumped or rolled through. TEAMS · FFAPick the fight type at the top of this screen. Up to four fighters share a wider arena and the camera follows the action. Everyone has a few lives (set them in Fight Options). Last fighter (or team) standing wins. Specials and supers hit everyone in their path, but never your teammate. SAMEPicking the same fighter as the CPU is fine: the CPU's copy wears different colors.

Keyboard: A/D or ←/→ walk, W/↑/space jump, S/↓ duck, J punch, K kick, L special, U super, I or Shift block (hold it and tap a direction to roll), P pause.
