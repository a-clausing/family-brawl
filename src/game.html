HOW TO PLAY

STICKSlide left or right to walk. Walking away is a little slower. JJump. Steer with the stick while you're in the air, and press P or K for a jump kick. AC can jump right over Brick to escape a corner. BTap or hold to block. Blocked hits only chip a little health. Double-tap B right next to an opponent who isn’t attacking or blocking to throw them (no damage, but they’re knocked down). SAMEPicking the same fighter as the CPU is fine: the CPU's copy wears different colors. 2V2 · FFAPick the fight type at the top of this screen. Up to four fighters share a wider arena and the camera follows the action. Everyone has a few lives (set them in Fight Options): lose your health bar and you drop back in; out of lives, you're out. Last fighter (or team) standing wins. Specials and supers hit everyone in their path, but never your teammate. SUPERFill the meter all the way (2.5 specials’ worth) without spending it and SUPER lights up. Supers can’t be blocked or jumped. P, KPunch and kick. Land a hit, then tap P or K again right away to chain a combo. SPECIALCosts 40 meter; the bar glows when it's ready. Jump or block the dumbbell, jump the quake, block the band (it reaches you even mid-jump), and jump Volt's Hyper Sprint (it can't be blocked).

Keyboard: left/right or A/D walk, up/W/space jump, J punch, K kick, L special, U super, I block, P pause.
