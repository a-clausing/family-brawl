Family Brawl — Goblin Character Specification

Character Overview

Name: Goblin
Fighting Style: Sneaky, fast, ranged trickster
Role: Hit-and-run fighter
Difficulty: Medium
Personality: Mischievous, aggressive, unpredictable

Goblin is a small, agile fighter who relies on quick attacks, evasive movement, and ranged weapons. He should feel faster than most characters but less durable.

Special Attack: Dagger Toss

Description: Goblin pulls out a rusty dagger and throws it horizontally toward his opponent.

Animation Sequence:

1. Goblin reaches behind his back and draws a dagger.
2. Pulls his throwing arm backward.
3. Quickly snaps his arm forward, releasing the dagger.
4. Dagger spins or travels point-first toward the opponent.
5. Goblin returns to his fighting stance.

Gameplay Mechanics:

* Fast-moving horizontal projectile.
* Moderate-low damage.
* Short startup and recovery.
* Can be blocked or jumped over.
* Dagger disappears upon impact or leaving the arena.
* Small impact spark when it hits an opponent.
* Intended for interrupting opponents and controlling distance.

Visual Effects: Silver dagger with brown handle, blue-white motion streaks, and a small yellow impact flash.

Super Attack: Poison Bomb

Description: Goblin pulls out a glowing green poison bomb and hurls it toward his opponent. Upon impact, the bomb explodes into a massive cloud of toxic green smoke.

Animation Sequence:

1. Goblin pulls out a bubbling green poison bomb.
2. Raises it overhead with a mischievous grin.
3. Winds up for a powerful throw.
4. Launches the bomb in an arc toward the opponent.
5. Bomb strikes the ground or opponent and explodes.
6. A bright green explosion expands into a large toxic cloud.
7. The cloud lingers briefly before dissipating.
8. Goblin returns to his fighting stance.

Gameplay Mechanics:

* High-damage area-of-effect attack.
* Slower startup than Dagger Toss.
* Bomb follows a curved trajectory.
* Explosion damages opponents within its radius.
* Applies a temporary poison damage-over-time effect on a successful hit.
* Poison cloud persists briefly as an area hazard.
* Opponents can avoid the initial explosion by moving away or timing a jump.
* No permanent poison effect.

Visual Effects: Round glass bomb containing glowing green liquid and a skull symbol, neon-green explosion, expanding toxic smoke, and floating green particles.

Basic Combat and Movement

* Idle: Subtle breathing, shifting weight, and twitching ears.
* Walk: Quick, hunched steps.
* Jump: Crouch, leap, airborne pose, landing.
* Duck: Low crouch with head tucked.
* Roll: Forward evasive tumble.
* Punch: Quick clawed jab or short-range punch.
* Kick: Fast forward kick with a short leg extension.
* Block: Raises both forearms to guard.
* Get Hit: Recoils backward with a brief impact reaction.
* Knocked Down: Falls backward and lands flat.
* Get Up: Rolls or pushes himself back to standing.
* Victory: Raises his arms triumphantly, laughing or brandishing his dagger.

Quick SWOT Analysis

Strengths

* Fast movement and evasive roll.
* Quick dagger projectile for ranged pressure.
* Poison Bomb controls space and punishes grouped or stationary opponents.
* Small character silhouette.

Weaknesses

* Lower health than heavyweight fighters.
* Short reach on punches and kicks.
* Poison Bomb has a noticeable startup.
* Relies on positioning and timing rather than raw damage.

Opportunities

* Strong hit-and-run combos using Dagger Toss.
* Can force opponents to move with poison-cloud area denial.
* Roll can help escape pressure and reposition.
* Rewards skilled players who manage spacing.

Threats

* Long-range fighters can keep Goblin at a distance.
* Heavy fighters can punish him severely if caught.
* Opponents who jump frequently can evade his dagger.
* Poorly timed Poison Bomb leaves him vulnerable.

Implementation Notes for Claude Code

Use the supplied Goblin sprite sheet as the visual reference.

* Maintain Goblin’s proportions, armor, and green skin.
* All sprites face right; mirror horizontally when facing left.
* Keep animation frames consistently scaled and grounded.
* Extract dagger, poison bomb, explosion, and smoke effects as separate assets.
* Use the same input mappings, animation system, hitbox conventions, and super-meter mechanics as existing Family Brawl characters.
* Do not introduce new controls.
* Match existing character balance conventions before assigning exact damage, cooldowns, or poison duration.
* Ensure the poison damage-over-time effect cannot stack indefinitely.
* Avoid making Goblin’s small sprite automatically immune to attacks intended to hit crouching opponents.

Design Goal: Goblin should feel like a quick, mischievous fighter who wins through mobility, projectiles, and clever positioning rather than overwhelming strength.
