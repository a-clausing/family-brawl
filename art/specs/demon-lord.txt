Character Description

Demon Lord is a terrifying, muscular demonic warrior with dark crimson and charcoal skin, enormous curved horns, glowing orange eyes, sharp teeth, jagged armor, and fiery orange cracks running across his body.

He is an aggressive, powerful fighter who combines strong physical attacks with dark magic and devastating hellfire abilities.

Fighting Style: Demonic magic bruiser
Strength: Very High
Defense: High
Speed: Medium
Jump: Medium
Magic Range: Medium to Long

Demon Lord should feel like a Level 9 boss character without being unfairly overpowered.

SPECIAL MOVE: SOUL GRASP

Demon Lord summons a large spectral demonic hand made of dark red and crimson energy. The hand flies forward, grabs an opponent, and pulls them toward Demon Lord.

Animation Sequence

1. Demon Lord plants his feet and raises one clawed hand.
2. Dark crimson energy begins swirling around his arm.
3. He thrusts his hand forward.
4. A large ghostly demonic hand launches horizontally toward the opponent.
5. If the hand connects, it grabs the opponent.
6. The spectral hand retracts, dragging the opponent toward Demon Lord.
7. Demon Lord releases the magical grip and returns to his fighting stance.

Gameplay Mechanics

* Range: Medium, approximately 4 character widths.
* Damage: Low initial damage.
* Effect: Pulls the opponent toward Demon Lord.
* On Hit: Briefly immobilizes the opponent while the hand retracts.
* On Release: Opponent ends up directly in front of Demon Lord, within punching distance.
* On Block: The hand dissipates without grabbing the opponent.
* On Miss: The hand disappears at maximum range.
* Cooldown: Slightly longer than a standard projectile special to compensate for its powerful positioning advantage.

The opponent should be vulnerable to a follow-up attack after being pulled in, but should not remain permanently stunned.

Use the separate spectral hand sprites from the supplied sprite sheet.

Important: Soul Grasp is NOT a normal damaging projectile. Its primary purpose is grabbing and pulling opponents toward Demon Lord.

SUPER MOVE: INFERNAL APOCALYPSE

Demon Lord opens a fiery portal above the battlefield and summons a devastating meteor shower that rains hellfire across the arena.

Animation Sequence

1. Demon Lord plants his feet and raises both arms.
2. His eyes glow brighter as crimson flames begin surrounding his body.
3. He spreads his arms outward and enters a dramatic POWER POSE.
4. A large circular red-orange portal opens in the sky above the arena.
5. Several flaming meteors begin falling diagonally toward the battlefield.
6. Meteors strike different locations, creating fiery explosions.
7. A final enormous meteor crashes down, creating a massive explosion of fire and debris.
8. The portal closes, the flames dissipate, and Demon Lord returns to his fighting stance.

Gameplay Mechanics

Attack Type: Multi-hit area attack.

Range: Entire arena.

Damage: Very High if multiple meteors connect.

Meteor Behavior:

* Summon 5–7 meteors during the attack.
* Meteors fall diagonally from above.
* Spread impacts across the battlefield rather than targeting one exact spot repeatedly.
* Each impact creates a brief explosion.
* Show a short warning indicator at each impact location before the meteor lands.
* Meteors can hit opponents individually.
* Apply a short hit-stun period to prevent unfair repeated stun-locking.
* The final meteor creates a larger explosion with stronger knockback.
* The attack should not automatically guarantee a hit.

Defending:

* Opponents can avoid meteors by moving away from marked impact locations.
* Blocking reduces damage and knockback.
* The super should be dangerous but still possible to defend against.

Visual Effects

* Red-orange circular portal overhead.
* Falling flaming meteors with fiery trails.
* Large explosions on impact.
* Burning particles and scattered embers.
* Brief screen shake during major impacts.
* Stronger screen shake for the final meteor.

Use the separate meteor, explosion, and hellfire sprites from the supplied sprite sheet.

Important: Infernal Apocalypse is a meteor shower, NOT a ground shockwave or single fireball. The meteors must fall from above and strike multiple positions throughout the arena.

BALANCING

Demon Lord should be one of the strongest characters in the monster roster, but not unbeatable.

Strengths:

* High physical damage.
* Excellent ability to control opponent positioning.
* Powerful full-arena super.
* Strong close-range combat.

Weaknesses:

* Soul Grasp is punishable when blocked or missed.
* Medium movement speed.
* Super has a noticeable startup animation.
* Meteor impact locations are telegraphed.
* Limited long-range pressure outside his super.

CHARACTER IDENTITY

Demon Lord is an aggressive demonic powerhouse who controls opponents with dark magic and unleashes catastrophic hellfire.

His Soul Grasp pulls opponents into close combat.

His Infernal Apocalypse transforms the battlefield into a meteor-filled inferno.

His Demonic Ascension victory animation showcases his overwhelming power.

Make his attacks visually dramatic, powerful, and distinct from Imp, Iron Ogre, Cave Troll, and Runic Golem.
