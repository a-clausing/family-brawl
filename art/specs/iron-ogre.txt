Character Description

Iron Ogre is a massive, muscular, gray-skinned monster with glowing orange eyes, large tusks, battle scars, and heavy iron armor covered in spikes. He wears rugged leather and fur clothing.

He is a slow, powerful heavyweight fighter who specializes in close-range attacks, high damage, and knocking opponents backward.

Fighting Style: Heavyweight bruiser
Strength: Very High
Defense: High
Speed: Low
Jump: Low
Attack Range: Short to Medium

SPECIAL MOVE: Shoulder Hit

Iron Ogre lowers his body, leans forward, and charges toward his opponent with his spiked iron shoulder armor.

Animation:

1. Ogre plants his feet and lowers his shoulder.
2. Briefly pauses to prepare the charge.
3. Explosively lunges forward, leading with his armored shoulder.
4. His shoulder spikes strike the opponent.
5. Opponent is knocked backward.
6. Ogre slides to a stop and returns to his fighting stance.

Gameplay Mechanics:

* Short-range forward charge.
* Travels approximately 1.5 character widths.
* Deals high damage if the shoulder connects.
* Causes strong knockback.
* Briefly stuns the opponent on a successful hit.
* Can be blocked, significantly reducing damage and preventing knockback.
* Has a short recovery period if the attack misses.
* No projectile or ranged damage.
* Include dust effects behind his feet during the charge and a sharp impact effect on contact.

SUPER MOVE: Axe Chop

Iron Ogre summons a massive, thick-handled iron battle axe and performs a devastating overhead chop. When the axe strikes the ground, it sends a destructive shockwave traveling forward across the arena.

Animation:

1. Ogre reaches for and grips his enormous iron axe.
2. Raises the axe above his head with both hands.
3. Briefly holds a dramatic POWER POSE, showing his strength.
4. Swings the axe downward with tremendous force.
5. Axe strikes the ground directly in front of him.
6. A large burst of rocks, dust, and orange energy erupts from the impact.
7. A ground shockwave travels horizontally toward the opponent.
8. Ogre pulls the axe free and returns to his normal stance.

Gameplay Mechanics:

* The initial axe strike deals massive damage at close range.
* The ground shockwave travels across the arena toward the opponent.
* Shockwave deals damage and knocks opponents backward.
* A direct axe hit deals more damage than the traveling shockwave.
* The shockwave stays close to the ground and can be avoided by jumping.
* Blocking reduces shockwave damage and prevents knockback.
* The super should feel extremely powerful, with screen shake when the axe hits the ground.
* Use the separate ground impact and shockwave effects from the sprite sheet.
* The axe is only used during his super and victory animations, not regular attacks.

VICTORY ANIMATION: Axe Raise

After winning, Iron Ogre celebrates by raising his massive iron axe triumphantly into the air.

Animation:

1. Ogre stands upright and pulls out his axe.
2. Raises the axe high overhead in one hand.
3. Holds the axe above his head while roaring victoriously.

Loop or hold the final victory pose until the match transitions.
