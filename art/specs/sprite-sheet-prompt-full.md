# Family Brawl: reusable sprite sheet prompt

Fill in the [brackets] and paste into the image generator. **Attach `style-reference.png`** (AC, Becky and B-Money as they look in the game) every time, so the new fighter matches their proportions and detail. The row order never changes, so every new sheet can be cut the same way.

```text
Create a 2D pixel-art fighting-game SPRITE SHEET for one character.

CHARACTER: [NAME] — [short description: body type, clothing, colors, personality]
SPECIAL MOVE: [name] — [what they do, and what they throw/send out, if anything]
SUPER MOVE: [name] — [what they do, step by step, and what it sends out]

STYLE AND PROPORTIONS (most important): match the attached reference image exactly. The fighters there are drawn in detailed, high-resolution modern pixel art with REALISTIC body proportions: about 7 heads tall, a normal-sized head, long legs, broad shoulders and an athletic build, like a modern 2D fighting game. Do NOT use chibi, super-deformed, big-head, stubby-legged, cute or classic 8-bit/16-bit sprite proportions. For a monster or animal, keep its natural real-life proportions instead of making it cute or big-headed. Same level of shading, outline thickness and detail as the reference.

BACKGROUND: one flat, solid [BRIGHT BLUE #0000FF / BRIGHT GREEN #00FF00 / BRIGHT MAGENTA #FF00FF] filling the entire image. No gradient, no texture, no floor, no shadows, no vignette. Pick the color that does NOT appear anywhere on the character or their effects.

STRICT RULES
- NO text, letters, numbers, labels, titles, arrows, frame borders, boxes, grid lines, dividers or logos anywhere in the image.
- Every frame shows the SAME character at the SAME size and scale, facing RIGHT.
- Leave a clear gap of background around every frame (at least a quarter of a frame's width); frames and effects must never touch or overlap each other.
- Nothing may be cut off at the edges of the image; leave a margin all around.
- In each row, all frames stand on the same invisible baseline (feet level), except jumps, rolls and falls.
- Effects (motion swooshes, sparks) stay close to the body in their own frame; thrown objects and big effects get their own separate frames.
- Crisp clean outlines, no soft glow bleeding into the background.

LAYOUT: 7 rows, left to right, in this exact order:
Row 1: IDLE (6 frames, fighting stance, slight breathing) | WALK (8 frames, a full step cycle)
Row 2: JUMP (6 frames: crouch to take off, rising, top, falling, landing, stand) | DUCK (3 frames: crouching low, head tucked) | BLOCK (2 frames: arms up guarding)
Row 3: ROLL (6 frames: tuck, tumble forward, come back up) | PUNCH (4 frames: guard, wind-up, full reach, back) | LOW JAB (3 frames: punching while ducking)
Row 4: KICK (4 frames: guard, chamber, full extension, back) | JUMP KICK (3 frames, in mid-air) | THROW (3 frames: grab, hold, toss) | HIT (3 frames: flinching backward)
Row 5: KNOCKED DOWN (4 frames: falling backward, lying flat on the ground) | GET UP (4 frames: from lying down back to standing) | VICTORY (3 frames: celebrating)
Row 6: SPECIAL (6 frames: the special move from wind-up to follow-through) | SPECIAL OBJECT FLYING (3 frames, the thrown thing alone, if any) | SPECIAL IMPACT (2 frames: burst or splash alone)
Row 7: SUPER (8 frames: wind-up, a big dramatic POWER POSE facing the viewer, release, recover) | SUPER OBJECT/EFFECT (3 frames, alone) | SUPER IMPACT (3 frames: big explosion or burst alone)

Wide landscape image, high resolution, consistent style across every frame.
```

## Tips
- Always attach `style-reference.png` (it's in the game's art notes, and in this chat). If a sheet still comes back with big heads and short legs, reply: "Redraw with realistic adult proportions like the reference, about 7 heads tall, not chibi."
- Background: blue for green/yellow/red characters, green for blue/purple/pink ones, magenta for green or blue ones. Never a color that's in the character, their clothes or their effects.
- If the generator crams it, ask for two images: Rows 1–5 (moves) and Rows 6–7 (Special and Super).
- Make the fighter card (with name and "SUPER: name") as a separate image.
- Before sending a sheet: no frames touching, no heads, hands or effects cut off.
